## Problem Statement

Job seekers in competitive, senior remote technology markets face a dual failure mode:

1. **The Spray-and-Pray Trap:** Modern AI job tools optimize for raw submission volume, blasting hundreds of generic resumes into applicant tracking systems (ATS). This triggers ATS anti-bot heuristics, exhausts recruiter goodwill, and yields near-zero conversion on high-value senior roles.
2. **The Manual Friction Sink:** The alternative—curating bespoke resumes, writing authentic cover letters, tracking follow-up cadences across fragmented email threads, and scheduling interview loops across time zones—demands 20-30 hours per week of repetitive administrative labor.

Existing commercial job tracking tools (Huntr, Teal, Simplify) are passive, generic, and bloated with bubbly SaaS tropes (cartoonish Kanban boards, pastel tags, low information density). They force users into manual status updates while leaking sensitive career data and salary expectations to third-party ad networks.

The real leverage in senior remote hiring is not brute-force volume, but **decision bandwidth and authentic craftsmanship**: scouting high-signal roles early, compiling mathematically tailored dossiers grounded in verified career milestones, and surfacing a high-density morning briefing for human authorization.

## What Makes This Cool

- **The Morning Executive Briefing:** Completely inverts the applicant dynamic. Instead of logging into job boards or moving cards on a Kanban board, the user begins each day reading an executive intelligence briefing that recaps open ends, active recruiter communications, urgent tasks, and lines up high-leverage decisions requiring 1-click authorization.
- **Zero-Hallucination Typst Precision Engine:** Rather than relying on LLMs to re-generate freeform PDFs from scratch, the system compiles resumes directly from a local `profile.yaml` via Typst. The LLM performs strictly scoped literary optimization—reordering, selecting, and emphasizing real, verified achievements to match role specs while preserving mathematical typographical perfection.
- **Infiltration Engine & Live War Room:** Scouts target engineering teams before public listings peak by analyzing public engineering blogs, GitHub repository commit spikes, and team expansions. For active interviews, it prepares background teleprompter briefs on the interviewers and company technical challenges.
- **Sovereign, Dark Editorial Aesthetic:** Rejects consumer SaaS conventions in favor of a classified intelligence dossier aesthetic: high-contrast serif typography, monospace operational telemetry, razor-thin borders, and deep charcoal surfaces.

## Constraints

- **Sovereign & Self-Hosted:** All career records, contact databases, API keys, and interview notes remain on the user's local machine in SQLite. Zero career data leaks to cloud tracking services.
- **Strict Human-in-the-Loop (V1):** The assistant is fully autonomous in discovery, parsing, drafting, and scheduling proposal generation, but strictly air-gapped from sending outbound communications or submitting applications without explicit 1-click human approval.
- **Editorial Design Language:** High-density, typography-first layout. No bubbly cards, no bright gradients, and no generic Kanban columns.
- **Sub-15s Document Compilation:** Typst compilation must execute locally in under 1 second, with end-to-end LLM dossier generation completing in under 15 seconds.

## Premises

1. **Quality over Volume:** 3-5 bespoke dossiers per day tailored with domain relevance and authentic proof points outperform 50 generic applications.
2. **Human-in-the-Loop Approval:** The agent acts as an executive chief of staff that discovers, drafts, and briefs; the user retains final executive dispatch authority.
3. **Sovereign Self-Hosted Architecture:** Local SQLite storage and private credentials guarantee absolute privacy and ownership of career materials.
4. **Editorial Interface:** High-density, typography-driven executive briefing layout is superior to generic task-board SaaS conventions.

## Approaches Considered

### Approach A: The Stager CLI & Editorial Briefing (Minimal Viable)
- **Summary:** Local Python/TypeScript CLI that ingests target board RSS/APIs, matches against a YAML profile, tailors Typst resumes via LLM structured outputs, and outputs a standalone static dark editorial `briefing.html` file.
- **Effort:** Small (human: ~3 days / CC: ~45 min)
- **Risk:** Low
- **Status:** Evaluated as a clean starter, but ruled out because the user desired real-time webhook handling, continuous scraping, and live interactive approval loops.

### Approach B: Sovereign Desk & Daemon (Ideal Architecture)
- **Summary:** Custom full-stack web application with an integrated background daemon running scrapers and email watchers, backed by SQLite and a Next.js/FastHTML frontend.
- **Effort:** Large (human: ~2 weeks / CC: ~3 hours)
- **Risk:** Medium
- **Status:** Ruled out for initial wedge due to high upfront plumbing overhead for custom scrapers and email protocol adapters.

### Approach C: n8n Engine + Bespoke Dossier App & Teleprompter (Lateral / Creative)
- **Summary:** Leverages self-hosted n8n container for scheduled scraping, webhook management, and inbox polling. Concentrates all custom software development on a bespoke dark editorial Dossier web app, the Typst resume compiler service, and the morning briefing synthesizer.
- **Effort:** Medium (human: ~1 week / CC: ~1.5 hours)
- **Risk:** Low-Medium
- **Status:** **Selected.** Maximizes velocity by delegating brittle scraping and email connectivity to proven open-source automation infrastructure while crafting a world-class editorial interface.

## Recommended Approach

**Approach C: Sovereign Engine + Bespoke Dossier App & Teleprompter.**

1. **Data Layer:** SQLite database storing normalized job leads, application states, interaction timelines, and interviewer dossiers. Includes schemas for `job_postings`, `applications`, `contacts`, `timeline_events`, and (for the Infiltration Engine) `target_repositories`, `blog_rss_feeds`, and `talking_points`.
2. **Profile Substrate:** Canonical `profile.yaml` containing structured career history, verified quantifiable metrics, target criteria, compensation thresholds, and deal-breaker constraints.
3. **Automation & Dispatch Subsystem (n8n):**
   - **Board Ingestion:** Scheduled cron workflows polling Greenhouse, Lever, and Ashby JSON/RSS endpoints.
   - **Inbound Communication:** IMAP watcher parsing inbound recruiter communications.
   - **1-Click Dispatch Transports:** When authorized via the UI, the Sovereign Desk Server triggers an n8n webhook executing targeted submission:
     - **Greenhouse:** Direct `POST https://boards-api.greenhouse.io/v1/boards/{board_token}/jobs/{job_id}` with `multipart/form-data` carrying mapped candidate JSON metadata and the compiled Typst PDF resume attachment.
     - **Ashby:** HTTP POST to `https://api.ashbyhq.com/posting-api/job-board/{organization}/application` with structured candidate fields and base64/multipart PDF resume.
     - **Lever:** HTTP POST to `https://api.lever.co/v0/postings/{site}/{posting_id}` with `multipart/form-data` containing application fields and the PDF resume attachment.
     - **Custom/Non-Standard Portals:** Fallback to a local headless Playwright script pre-filling application form fields, or an in-app 1-click clipboard staging modal with pre-formatted cover letters and responses.
4. **Document Compiler:** Local Typst service injecting tailored bullet points into a clean Harvard-style resume template, emitting crisp 1-page PDF artifacts.
5. **Editorial Dashboard:** Dark editorial web UI rendering the Morning Executive Briefing, Urgent Decision Queue, and Literary Bullet Diff.
6. **Infiltration Engine & Live War Room (Milestone 5):** Air-gapped real-time HUD (`/war-room.html`) and commit/RSS ingestion engine (`target-repo-watcher.json`). Monitors target team GitHub commit activity and engineering blog publications to identify unlisted architectural priorities and arm the user with live teleprompter talking points during interviews.
7. **Visual Wireframe Reference:** Captured wireframe sketch saved at `/var/folders/l3/3d4dp7750q9dqwzmw7ftcycr0000gn/T/gstack-sketch.On9hRL/sketch.png` (workspace copy at `.gstack/tmp/sketch.html`).

## Open Questions

1. **Non-Standard Portal Automation:** Greenhouse, Lever, and Ashby provide clean public JSON APIs. For Workday, Taleo, or bespoke forms, what is the best headless automation strategy (Playwright script triggered by n8n vs manual copy-paste)?
2. **Email Authentication Protocol:** Direct IMAP/SMTP credentials with app passwords versus local Google OAuth2 token rotation for Gmail integration.

## Success Criteria

1. **Daily Morning Briefing:** System delivers a consolidated briefing by 08:00 AM via local OS desktop notification (with terminal bell alert) linking directly to `http://localhost:3000`, recapping newly discovered roles scoring ≥85% match, urgent recruiter replies, and queued decisions.
2. **Typst Quality Bar:** 100% valid, single-page PDF generation in <1 second with zero typographical overflow or LLM hallucinations.
3. **Decision Velocity:** User can review a staged application, inspect the literary bullet diff, and authorize dispatch in under 60 seconds.
4. **Zero Cloud Leakage:** All candidate history and credentials remain strictly on local storage.
